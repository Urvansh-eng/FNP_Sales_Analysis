# 🎁 FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

An **Excel-based Sales Data Analytics project** built using the Ferns and Petals (FNP) dataset to analyze sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and delivery patterns.

The project transforms raw customer, order, and product data into an interactive dashboard with KPIs, PivotTables, charts, and slicers to support business decision-making.

![FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard](Dashboard_SS.png)

## 📌 Project Overview

Ferns and Petals (FNP) specializes in gifting products for occasions such as **Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine's Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries**.

The objective of this project was to analyze the available sales data and build a dashboard that answers important business questions around:

- Revenue performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Product performance
- Customer spending
- Order volume by city
- Delivery time
- Revenue by occasion and category
- Product popularity across occasions

## 🎯 Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer the following questions:

1. What is the overall revenue?
2. What is the average order and delivery time?
3. How does monthly sales performance fluctuate during 2023?
4. Which products generate the most revenue?
5. How much are customers spending on average?
6. How do the top 5 products perform?
7. Which are the top 10 cities by number of orders?
8. Does order quantity have an impact on delivery time?
9. How does revenue compare across different occasions?
10. Which products are most popular for specific occasions?

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses three primary datasets:

- `customers.csv` — Customer information
- `orders.csv` — Order, quantity, date, delivery, location, and occasion information
- `products.csv` — Product, category, price, occasion, and description information

The dataset contains:

- **100 customers**
- **1,000 orders**
- **70 products**
